I'm an enthusiastic IT student with a growing focus on web development, frontend engineering, and UI/UX design. What started as curiosity about how websites work has turned into a genuine passion for building interfaces that are both functional and enjoyable to use.

My approach blends creativity with problem-solving — I like breaking a design down into logical, reusable pieces, then polishing the details until an interface feels effortless. Every project, whether academic or personal, is a chance to learn a new tool, pattern, or way of thinking about the user.

Looking ahead, my goal is to grow into a well-rounded frontend developer who can take an idea from a rough sketch to a fully responsive, production-ready product — and eventually contribute to teams building meaningful software for real people.
